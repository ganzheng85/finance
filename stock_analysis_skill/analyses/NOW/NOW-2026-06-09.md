**Evidence of High Switching Costs:**
- ServiceNow is **mission-critical IT infrastructure** for 8,400+ enterprises
- Average customer has:
  - 1,000-5,000 workflows automated
  - 50-200 third-party integrations (ITSM tools, monitoring, cloud platforms)
  - 100-500 trained IT staff with ServiceNow expertise
  - 5-10 years of institutional knowledge embedded in platform
- Migration to alternative requires:
  - **2-3 year project timeline**
  - **$5-15M migration cost** (consulting, retraining, workflow recreation)
  - **High execution risk** (business disruption during migration)
  - **Opportunity cost** of IT resources on migration vs innovation

**Quantitative Evidence:**
- **98% renewal rate** (Q1 2026) - among highest in enterprise SaaS
- **Net dollar retention: 120-125%** - existing customers expanding spend
- **25% seat growth YoY** - customers adding users, not churning
- **630 customers with $5M+ ACV** (+22% YoY) - deep enterprise penetration

**Moat Durability:**
High switching costs moat is **durable over 5-7 years**, but faces long-term AI threat:
- **Near-term (2026-2028)**: Moat intact - migration complexity remains high even with AI tools
- **Medium-term (2029-2031)**: Moat tested - agentic AI may simplify migrations, lowering switching costs
- **Long-term (2032+)**: Moat uncertain - if AI agents make workflows "portable," switching costs erode

4. **Product mix shift**: Lower-margin AI products growing faster than high-margin subscriptions
