## Investment Thesis - Why BUY?

### 1. AWS Re-Acceleration Validates Massive AI Capex

**The Debate:**
- **Capex**: $177B annualized (Q1 2026) - up from ~$75B in 2023
- **FCF Impact**: Free cash flow dropped to $9.8B (from $36.8B in 2023)
- **Market Fear**: "Amazon is burning cash on AI infrastructure that won't pay off"

**The Reality:**
- ✅ **AWS revenue growth**: Re-accelerated to **20%+ YoY** (Q1 2026)
  - 2023: Decelerated to 12-15% (cloud optimization fears)
  - 2024: Stabilized at 17-19%
  - **Q1 2026: 20%+** (AI workloads driving demand)
- ✅ **AI infrastructure demand is real**: OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, enterprises migrating AI workloads to AWS
- ✅ **Bedrock (AI platform)**: Growing 200%+ YoY from small base
- ✅ **Custom AI chips**: Trainium/Inferentia offer 40% cost savings vs Nvidia GPUs

**Conclusion**: $177B capex is a **calculated bet** on multi-year AI infrastructure demand. Early evidence (AWS re-acceleration) supports the thesis.

### 2. Retail Margin Expansion Path is Clear

**North America Retail Operating Margin:**
- 2021: 5.2%
- 2024: 5.8%
- **Q1 2026: 6.2%** (best ever)
- **Target: 8-10%** by 2028-2030 (Walmart-like)

**Margin Drivers:**
1. **Automation**: Robotics in fulfillment centers (Kiva, Proteus) reducing labor costs
2. **Regionalization**: Reduced long-haul shipping (saved $2B in 2025)
3. **Advertising**: $47B revenue (2025), 20%+ margins, improving blended margins
4. **Prime Fee Increase**: $149/year (from $139) adds high-margin subscription revenue

**Impact**: Every **1% margin improvement** = **$6B+ additional operating income**

**Current Trajectory**: 6.2% → 7% by late 2026 → 8% by 2028

### 3. Exceptional Moat (16/18) - Top-Tier Competitive Position

**Moat Scorecard:**
- **Brand Power**: 3/3 (Prime 230M members, #2 global brand value)
- **Network Effects**: 3/3 (Marketplace flywheel, AWS ecosystem, 500M Alexa devices)
- **Cost Advantages**: 3/3 (Scale economies, fulfillment network, AWS infrastructure)
- **Switching Costs**: 3/3 (Prime lock-in, AWS migration costs, seller relationships)
- **Regulatory/IP**: 2/3 (13,000+ patents, but antitrust risk)
- **Data Moat**: 2/3 (310M+ accounts, recommendation engine, Alexa voice data)

**Key Metrics:**
- **Prime retention**: 98%+ (annual churn <2%)
- **AWS customer retention**: 95%+ (switching costs 12-24 months migration)
- **Marketplace sellers**: 2M+ active (inventory, reviews, relationships tied to Amazon)

**Conclusion**: Competitive position is **strengthening**, not eroding.

## Key Issues and How Management is Solving Them

### Issue #1: Massive Capex Straining Free Cash Flow

**Problem:**
- Capex: $177B annualized (Q1 2026)
- FCF: $9.8B (down 73% from $36.8B in 2023)
- 4-quarter rolling FCF yield: 0.37% (very low)

**Management Solution:**
- Andy Jassy: "We're investing to meet customer demand. AI workloads are growing 3x faster than traditional cloud."
- AWS re-acceleration to 20%+ validates capex thesis
- Guidance: Capex to remain elevated through 2027, then moderate
- Promise: AI infrastructure ROI will exceed historical cloud capex returns

**Confidence**: Medium-High (AWS re-acceleration provides early validation)

### Issue #2: Retail Margin Expansion Still Below Peers

**Problem:**
- North America retail margin: 6.2% (vs Walmart 4.5%, but Walmart has lower gross margins)
- International retail: Still unprofitable (-1.2% margin)
- Amazon should achieve 8-10% margins at current scale

**Management Solution:**
- Automation: Robotics reducing labor costs
- Regionalization: $2B savings in 2025 from reduced long-haul shipping
- Advertising: $47B revenue (2025) at 20%+ margins improving blended margins
- Prime fee increases: $149/year adds high-margin subscription revenue

**Confidence**: High (clear path to 7-8% margins by 2027-2028)

### Issue #3: Regulatory Overhang (Antitrust)

**Problem:**
- FTC lawsuit (filed 2023) alleges marketplace dominance abuse
- EU fined Amazon €2.4B (2024) for self-preferencing

**Management Solution:**
- Amazon denies wrongdoing, argues sellers choose FBA for customer convenience
- Investing in legal defense, not changing business practices
- Lobbying: $21M/year federal lobbying (2025)

**Confidence**: Medium (fines likely $5-10B, breakup unlikely 5-10% probability)
