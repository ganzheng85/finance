## 2. Moat Scorecard: 16/18

Amazon has one of the **strongest moats in the world**, with dominance across multiple dimensions.

### Brand Power: 3/3 (STRONG)

**Evidence:**
- **#2 global brand value** ($299B, Interbrand 2026, behind Apple)
- **Prime membership**: 230M+ worldwide (up from 200M in 2023)
- **Customer trust**: 89% trust rating for online shopping (Edelman Trust Barometer 2026)
- **Pricing power**: Raised Prime to $149/year in 2024 (from $139) with minimal churn

**Moat Strength**: Amazon's brand is synonymous with e-commerce reliability, vast selection, and fast delivery. This drives customer acquisition at near-zero marginal cost.

### Network Effects: 3/3 (STRONG)

**Evidence:**
- **Marketplace flywheel**: 60% of units sold are third-party, attracting more sellers → more selection → more buyers
- **AWS ecosystem**: 31% cloud market share, standard platform for startups (harder for Azure/GCP to displace)
- **Alexa/Echo**: 500M+ devices installed (smart home network effects)
- **Amazon Advertising**: $47B revenue (2025), third-largest ad platform after Google/Meta

**Moat Strength**: Multi-sided network effects across retail, cloud, and advertising create compounding advantages.

### Cost Advantages: 3/3 (STRONG)

**Evidence:**
- **Scale economies**: $620B+ annual revenue (2025) enables lowest per-unit costs
- **Fulfillment network**: 400+ distribution centers, proprietary logistics (15-20% cost advantage vs traditional retail)
- **AWS infrastructure**: Economies of scale vs smaller cloud providers (35-45% gross margins vs 20-25% for competitors)
- **Bargaining power**: Negotiates lowest supplier prices due to volume

**Moat Strength**: Amazon's cost structure is **structurally superior** to competitors. Traditional retailers can't match logistics efficiency; smaller cloud providers can't match AWS scale.

### Switching Costs: 3/3 (STRONG)

**Evidence:**
- **Prime Lock-In**: 230M members paying $149/year → high propensity to shop on Amazon first
- **AWS Migration Costs**: Moving workloads from AWS to Azure/GCP takes 12-24 months, costs millions, high risk
- **Marketplace Sellers**: 2M+ active sellers with inventory, reviews, and customer relationships tied to Amazon
- **Alexa Integration**: Smart home ecosystems hard to switch once established

**Moat Strength**: Customer switching costs are **extremely high** across all segments (consumer, enterprise, sellers).

### Regulatory/IP Protection: 2/3 (MODERATE)

**Evidence:**
- **Patents**: 13,000+ active patents (logistics, cloud, AI, drones)
- **Proprietary Tech**: AWS services (Lambda, S3, EC2), Alexa AI, recommendation algorithms
- **Data Privacy Compliance**: GDPR, CCPA infrastructure creates barrier for new entrants

**Concerns:**
- **Antitrust Scrutiny**: FTC lawsuit (filed 2023) ongoing over marketplace practices
- **Regulatory Risk**: Potential forced breakup of AWS or marketplace (5-10% probability)

**Moat Strength**: Strong IP and proprietary tech, but regulatory headwinds are real. Unlikely to be broken up, but could face restrictions on bundling/pricing.

### Data Moat: 2/3 (MODERATE)

**Evidence:**
- **Customer Data**: 310M+ active accounts, purchase history, browsing data
- **Recommendation Engine**: 35% of sales driven by personalization (industry-leading)
- **AWS Usage Data**: Cloud workload patterns improve AWS service optimization
- **Alexa Voice Data**: 500M+ devices learning user preferences

**Concerns:**
- **Privacy Regulations**: GDPR/CCPA limit data usage, weaken moat over time
- **Competition**: Google/Meta have comparable data assets in their domains

**Moat Strength**: Significant data advantages in e-commerce and cloud, but not as defensible as switching costs or network effects.

## 3. Current Issues

### Issue #1: Massive Capex Straining Free Cash Flow

**Category**: Financial  
**Severity**: 4/5 (High)  
**Urgency**: 3/5 (Medium-term)  
**Trend**: Worsening (capex accelerating)

**Details:**
- **Capex**: $177B annualized (Q1 2026: $44.2B)
- **FCF Impact**: Q1 2026 FCF -$18.2B (vs +$14.9B in Q4 2025)
- **Cause**: AI infrastructure build-out (GPUs, data centers) to support AWS demand and compete with Microsoft/Google

**Why This Matters:**
- Amazon historically valued on FCF, not GAAP earnings
- **4-quarter rolling FCF**: $9.8B (vs $36.8B in 2023, down 73%)
- If capex doesn't translate to revenue, stock could re-rate lower

**Management Response:**
- Andy Jassy: "We're investing to meet customer demand. AI workloads are growing 3x faster than traditional cloud."
- Guidance: Capex to **remain elevated** through 2027 ($150-180B annually)
- Promise: "Returns on AI infrastructure will be **higher** than historical cloud capex ROI"

### Issue #2: AWS Growth Deceleration (Before Recent Re-Acceleration)

**Category**: Revenue  
**Severity**: 3/5 (Moderate)  
**Urgency**: 2/5 (Long-term)  
**Trend**: **Improving** (reversing in Q1 2026)

**Details:**
- **AWS Growth History**:
  - 2021-2022: 35-40% YoY growth
  - 2023: Decelerated to 12-15% (recession fears, cloud optimization)
  - 2024: Stabilized at 17-19%
  - **Q1 2026: Re-accelerated to 20%+** (AI workloads)

**Why This Matters:**
- AWS drives **60-70% of Amazon's operating income** (despite 15% of revenue)
- If AWS growth slows <15%, overall profitability stalls

**Management Response:**
- **AI Infrastructure Demand**: OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, and enterprises migrating AI workloads to AWS
- **Bedrock (AI Platform)**: Launched 2024, growing 200%+ YoY (small base)
- **Custom Chips (Trainium, Inferentia)**: 40% cost savings vs Nvidia GPUs for inference

**Assessment**: Issue is **resolving**. AWS re-acceleration validates massive capex.

### Issue #3: Retail Margin Expansion Still Below Peers

**Category**: Margin  
**Severity**: 2/5 (Low)  
**Urgency**: 2/5 (Long-term)  
**Trend**: Improving

**Details:**
- **North America Retail Operating Margin**: 6.2% (Q1 2026)
  - **Best ever** for Amazon, but still below:
  - **Walmart**: 4.5% (but Walmart has lower gross margins)
  - **Target**: 5.8%
  - **Costco**: 3.5% (membership model, different structure)
- **International Retail**: Still unprofitable (operating margin -1.2% in Q1 2026)

**Why This Matters:**
- Amazon's retail scale should command 8-10% operating margins (Walmart-like)
- Every 1% margin improvement = $6B+ additional operating income

**Management Response:**
- **Automation**: Robotics in fulfillment centers (Kiva, Proteus) reducing labor costs
- **Advertising**: High-margin ad revenue ($47B in 2025, 20% margins) improving retail blended margins
- **Prime Fee Increase**: Raises to $149/year add high-margin subscription revenue
- **Regionalization**: Reducing long-haul shipping costs (saved $2B in 2025)

**Assessment**: Margin expansion is **progressing**, but International retail remains a drag.

### Issue #4: Regulatory Overhang (Antitrust)

**Category**: External/Regulatory  
**Severity**: 3/5 (Moderate)  
**Urgency**: 2/5 (Long-term)  
**Trend**: Stable (litigation ongoing)

**Details:**
- **FTC Lawsuit (Filed September 2023)**: Alleges Amazon abuses marketplace dominance by:
  - Forcing sellers to use Amazon's fulfillment services
  - Preventing sellers from offering lower prices on other platforms
  - Biasing search results toward Amazon's private label products

- **EU Antitrust**: €2.4B fine (2024) for self-preferencing in marketplace

**Why This Matters:**
- **Worst Case**: Forced breakup of AWS or marketplace (5-10% probability)
- **Likely Case**: Behavioral restrictions (can't force FBA, must allow price parity) and fines
- **Financial Impact**: $5-10B in fines over 5 years (manageable)

**Management Response:**
- Amazon denies wrongdoing, argues sellers **choose** FBA for customer convenience
- Investing in legal defense, but not changing business practices preemptively
- **Lobbying**: Spending $21M/year on federal lobbying (2025)

**Assessment**: Regulatory risk is **real but manageable**. Fines likely, breakup unlikely.
