### 3. Unique Competitive Position: Custom Chips + AI Networking (Both Sides)

**The Broadcom Advantage:**

Most investors think: "Broadcom vs NVIDIA in AI chips"

**Wrong framing.** Broadcom and NVIDIA serve different markets:

| Company | Product | Customers | Business Model | Revenue/Rack |
|---------|---------|-----------|----------------|--------------|
| **NVIDIA** | Merchant GPUs (H100, GB200) | Everyone | Sell GPUs at retail | $2-3M |
| **Broadcom** | Custom ASICs (TPU, MTIA) | 6 hyperscalers | Co-design + networking | **$6-8M** |

**Broadcom's 2x-3x Revenue Advantage per AI Rack:**
1. **Custom AI Chip**: $2-3M per rack (TPU, MTIA design + TSMC fabrication)
2. **Tomahawk Switches**: $500K-1M (networking silicon connecting chips)
3. **Optical Interconnect**: $300-500K (photonics for data center connectivity)
4. **Total**: **$3-4.5M per rack** (vs NVIDIA's $2-3M GPU-only)

**Example: Google Data Center**
- Uses Broadcom TPU chips (custom AI accelerators)
- Uses Broadcom Tomahawk switches (networking)
- Uses Broadcom optical interconnect (connectivity)
- **Broadcom captures 3x revenue per rack vs competitors**

**Conclusion**: Broadcom has a **unique competitive moat** - neither NVIDIA nor Marvell can match this dual position.
