## Part 5: The Behavioral Finance Truth

### Why People Hold Cash (The Real Reasons)

Research shows people hold cash for emotional, not rational, reasons:

**1. Loss Aversion**
- Fear of buying at the top
- Fear of looking stupid if market drops tomorrow
- "I'll wait for certainty" (which never comes)

**2. Regret Minimization**
- "If I wait and market drops, I'll be smart"
- "If I buy and market drops, I'll be stupid"
- Better to do nothing and avoid regret

**3. Sunk Cost Fallacy**
- "I've been in cash for 3 months, if I deploy now I 'lost' those 3 months"
- "I'll wait a bit more to make up for lost time"
- This compounds the problem

### The Fix: Rules-Based Deployment

**Remove emotion with a systematic plan:**

```
Cash Deployment Rules (Example for $200K):

Week 1: 
- Transfer $20K to VOO (10%)
- No matter what market does

Week 2-4:
- Nothing, let it ride

Month 2:
- Transfer $20K to VOO (10%)
- Total deployed: $40K (20%)

Months 3-6:
- $20K/month to VOO
- By month 6: $120K deployed (60%)

Dip Reserve:
- Keep $40K for S&P drops
- -10%: Deploy $15K
- -15%: Deploy $15K
- -20%: Deploy $10K

Trading Reserve:
- Keep $40K for opportunities
- Only deploy on high-conviction setups
- Can stay in cash indefinitely
```

**This way:**
- You're systematically reducing cash (no FOMO)
- You have dip-buying power (no regret)
- You have trading reserves (keep your edge)
- You remove emotion (it's automatic)
