## Why Are They Cheap But Still Dropping?

### Paradox: Good Valuations, Bad Price Action

**The Disconnect:**
```
Valuation says: "MSFT/META are fairly priced to cheap"
Price action says: "Market is selling them aggressively"
```

**Why?**

### 1. **Forward-Looking Concerns**

**Market is NOT valuing based on TRAILING P/E:**
- Trailing P/E = what they EARNED in past 12 months
- Forward P/E = what they're EXPECTED to earn next 12 months

**Current fears:**
- MSFT: Azure growth slowing from 30% → 25%?
- META: Ad revenue headwinds, Reality Labs losses
- Both: AI revenue not materializing in 2026

**If earnings disappoint, P/E will EXPAND:**
```
MSFT Example:
  Current: $379 / $16.78 EPS = 22.6x P/E
  
  If EPS drops to $15: $379 / $15 = 25.3x P/E (more expensive!)
  If price drops to $330: $330 / $15 = 22x P/E (maintain valuation)
```

**Market is pricing in EARNINGS RISK, not current P/E**

---

### 2. **Sector Rotation Trumps Valuation**

**Semiconductors are more expensive but rallying:**

| Stock | P/E | Growth | PEG | Today's Change |
|-------|-----|--------|-----|----------------|
| **MSFT** | 22.6x | 23% | 1.22 | -4.1% ⬇️ |
| **META** | 20.6x | 61% | 0.87 | -5.2% ⬇️ |
| **NVDA** | ~60x | 80% | 0.75 | -0.4% ➡️ |
| **AMD** | ~100x | 60% | 1.67 | +2.4% ⬆️ |

**Paradox:**
- MSFT/META cheaper on P/E but SELLING
- NVDA/AMD expensive on P/E but BUYING

**Why?**
- **Momentum trumps valuation** in short-term
- Semiconductors = "hot sector" (AI infrastructure)
- Mega-cap tech = "crowded trade unwinding"
- Market rotates AWAY from value INTO momentum

**This is why "doing nothing" is actually reasonable:**
> Valuation eventually matters. Sector rotations reverse.
> If you hold 12 months, MSFT/META at 22.6x/20.6x P/E will likely outperform
> But in next 1-3 months, momentum could keep driving them lower
