# Dip Buying & Price Drop Trading Skill

A systematic framework for buying stocks and ETFs when prices drop, helping you distinguish between bargains and value traps.

## What This Skill Does

This skill helps you answer the critical question: **"The stock is down 20-30%. Should I buy?"**

It provides:
1. ✅ **5-Point Bargain Test** - Quickly assess if a drop is a buying opportunity or value trap
2. 📊 **Position Sizing Framework** - How much to buy initially (1/3-1/3-1/3 rule)
3. ⬇️ **Continued Drop Playbook** - What to do if it keeps falling (average down? hold? exit?)
4. ⬆️ **Bounce Management** - When to take profits as price recovers
5. 🛡️ **Risk Management** - Stop-losses, position limits, emotional discipline rules

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when:
- A stock you own or watch drops -15% to -50% suddenly
- You want to capitalize on market selloffs
- You're considering "buying the dip" but unsure if it's smart
- You need a disciplined framework to avoid emotional decisions
- You want to distinguish temporary fear from fundamental deterioration

## Core Philosophy

**Not all dips are buying opportunities.**

The skill teaches you to distinguish:
- **Bargain** = Temporary drop, fundamentals intact, overreaction → BUY
- **Value Trap** = Permanent impairment, fundamentals broken → AVOID

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Run the 5-Point Bargain Test
Ask these 5 questions:
1. Is the business intact? (Moat, revenue, margins, balance sheet)
2. Is management credible? (Track record, transparency, action plan)
3. Is valuation compelling? (30%+ discount to fair value?)
4. Is risk-reward favorable? (3:1+ upside/downside asymmetry?)
5. Is there a catalyst for recovery? (Within 3-6 months?)

**Score**: 5/5 = Strong Buy | 4/5 = Buy | 3/5 = Watch | 2/5 = Avoid | 0-1/5 = Sell

### Step 2: Enter in Stages (1/3 - 1/3 - 1/3)
- **First 33%**: Buy after initial stabilization
- **Second 33%**: Buy on bounce (+10%) OR deeper drop (-15%)
- **Third 33%**: Buy when catalyst confirms OR value is absurd

### Step 3: Manage the Position
- **If it keeps dropping**: Reassess thesis. Intact? Add more. Broken? Exit.
- **If it bounces**: Sell 25% at +30%, sell 50% at fair value, hold 25% with trailing stop.

## Example: Meta (META) Q4 2022 Drop

**Situation**: Meta dropped from $145 to $90 (-38%) on Q4 2022 earnings miss, metaverse losses.

**Bargain Test Score**: 4.5/5
- Business intact: ✅ (3B users, dominant in social)
- Management credible: ⚠️ (Zuck admitted mistakes, "year of efficiency")
- Valuation compelling: ✅ (10x P/E vs 20-30x history, 40% discount)
- Risk-reward favorable: ✅ (3-5:1 asymmetry)
- Catalyst: ✅ (Q1 2023 earnings, AI integration)

**Entry**: Bought at $90, $102, $115 (avg $102, 4.5% portfolio)

**Outcome**: Stock hit $190 by June 2023 (+86% from avg cost), $475 by late 2024 (+366%)

**Lesson**: Buying quality on temporary fear = high returns.

## Anti-Patterns (What NOT to Do)

❌ "It's down 30%, must be cheap!" - Run Bargain Test first  
❌ Average down blindly - Only if thesis intact AND position <10%  
❌ Wait for the bottom - You can't time it, buy on stabilization  
❌ Sell at breakeven - Sell on thesis validation (profit) or break (loss)  
❌ Revenge trading - Don't FOMO re-buy after panic selling  

## Position Sizing Rules

- **Max single position**: 10% of portfolio
- **Max sector exposure**: 25% of portfolio
- **Max dip-buying allocation**: 40-50% of portfolio
- **Cash reserve**: Keep 20-30% dry powder for more opportunities

## Risk Management

**Stop Losses**:
- **Time stop**: Exit if no progress in 12-18 months
- **Price stop**: Exit at -40-50% from average cost (hard stop)
- **Fundamental stop**: Exit if dividend cut, credit downgrade, CEO resignation, accounting fraud

**Profit Taking**:
- Sell 25% at +20-30% gain (lock in quick win)
- Sell 50% at fair value (book most gains)
- Hold 25% with trailing stop-loss (20% from peak)

## Success Metrics

Track your dip buying performance:
- **Win rate**: % of dip buys that returned >20% within 18 months
- **Avg return**: Average return per dip buy position
- **Max drawdown**: Largest loss from peak on any single position
- **Bargain Test accuracy**: Of positions scoring 4-5/5, what % worked?

**Target Metrics** (aspirational):
- Win rate: 60-70% (you'll have losers, that's OK)
- Avg return: +30-50% per winner over 12-24 months
- Max drawdown: <-40% (cut losers before -50%)
- Bargain Test accuracy: 70%+ (4-5/5 scores should work 70%+ of time)

## Common Mistakes (Learn from Others)

1. **Catching falling knives** - Buying without waiting for stabilization
   - Fix: Wait 2-3 days after drop, watch volume/technicals

2. **Averaging down on value traps** - Doubling down as fundamentals deteriorate
   - Fix: Re-run Bargain Test every -10-15% drop. If score falls to 2/5, exit.

3. **Selling winners too early** - Locking in +20% gain, missing +200% rally
   - Fix: Use 3-tier exit (25% quick, 50% at fair value, 25% runner)

4. **Holding losers too long** - "It'll come back eventually" (sunk cost fallacy)
   - Fix: Time stop (12-18 months) and price stop (-40-50%)

5. **Over-concentrating** - Putting 30% of portfolio in one dip buy
   - Fix: Max 10% per position, 25% per sector, 50% total dip allocation
