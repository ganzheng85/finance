5. Primary Issue Identification

Issue: Extreme AI Capex with Uncertain ROI Timeline

What's Happening:
Meta is spending $125-145B annually (30-35% of revenue) on AI infrastructure, consuming all incremental operating cash flow and forcing suspension of share buybacks. This represents a strategic bet that AI infrastructure investment today will generate outsized returns by 2027-2028.

Why It Matters:
- FCF Flat: Despite revenue +54% over 2 years, free cash flow unchanged at ~$50B annually
- Shareholder Returns Halted: $26B buybacks in 2025 → $0 in 2026
- Balance Sheet Shift: Raised $30B debt to fund AI, moving from net cash to net debt
- Opportunity Cost: Could instead return $100B+ to shareholders over 2026-2027

Root Causes:
1. AI Arms Race: Belief that winner-take-most dynamics require infrastructure dominance NOW
2. Data Center Lead Times: GPU clusters and facilities take 2-3 years to build, forcing early commitment
3. Competitive Pressure: Google, Amazon, Microsoft all increasing AI capex; Meta matching or exceeding
4. Network Effects in AI: More data → better AI → more users → more data (reinforcing loop requires scale)

Market Reaction:
Stock down 27% from highs as investors demand proof that $400B+ in AI capex (cumulative 2024-2027) will generate returns exceeding cost of capital.

If AI capex succeeds (2027-2028 inflection):

- FCF inflects to $90-110B (nearly double current)
- Buybacks resume at $30-50B/year

If AI capex fails (no inflection by 2028):
- $400B+ capex destroyed without returns
- FCF remains suppressed
- No shareholder returns for 5+ years
- Stock declines to $400-450 (-30-35%)
