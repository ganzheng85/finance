Meta is trading at **LOW levels** (43% in 52-week range, -20% from highs) despite delivering exceptional 33% revenue growth in Q1 2026 and overtaking Google as the world's largest digital advertising platform. The stock has declined primarily due to investor concerns over aggressive AI capital expenditure ($125-145B in 2026) and massive Reality Labs losses ($4B/quarter). Management **CAN SOLVE** the core advertising business issues but has demonstrated poor capital discipline in Reality Labs. **Financial resilience is ADEQUATE** - fortress balance sheet but FCF turning negative due to unprecedented AI capex. This represents a **high-quality business at a temporary discount**, offering 35-50% upside if AI investments prove productive.
