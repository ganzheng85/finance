## 2. Moat Analysis: Score 14/18 (STRONG)

### Brand Power: 3/3 (STRONG)
- **30%+ U.S. QSR pizza market share** - Dominant category leader
- **19.8% total U.S. pizza market** - Targeting 50% QSR share
- **37.3 million active loyalty members** (Domino's Rewards)
- Global recognition across 90+ countries, 21,000+ stores
- Consistent brand positioning around value, speed, and technology

**Evidence:** Brand consistently wins on delivery speed (26min 10sec average, industry-leading) and value perception. Social media presence and marketing effectiveness demonstrated by loyalty program growth.

### Network Effects: 2/3 (MODERATE-STRONG)
- **"Fortressing" strategy** - Dense store clusters reduce delivery radius
- **21,000+ global stores** create geographic coverage moat
- More stores → faster delivery → better experience → more customers → support more stores
- Integration with DoorDash and Uber Eats (5%+ of U.S. sales) expands reach

**Limitation:** Not pure two-sided network effect like Uber/Airbnb. Adding stores improves service but doesn't exponentially increase value.

### Cost Advantages: 3/3 (STRONG)
- **Vertically integrated supply chain** - 27 dough manufacturing/distribution centers
- **Scale procurement** - Massive buying power on cheese, flour, toppings
- **Franchisee AUV: $1.3M+** vs competitors ($1.1M Pizza Hut, $900K Papa John's)
- **Technology amortization** - $150M+ annual R&D spread over 21K stores = $7K/store
- **Own-driver model** vs third-party delivery = superior unit economics

**Evidence:** Gross margins 40%, operating margins 19.43% despite intense competition. Company-owned store margins compressed to 10.1% (from 15.5%) due to insurance/labor, but franchise model shields corporate.

### Switching Costs: 2/3 (MODERATE)
- **Loyalty program lock-in** - 37.3M active users accumulate points
- **Mobile app integration** - 70%+ digital ordering creates habit formation
- **Stored preferences** - Saved orders, payment info, favorite locations

**Limitation:** Switching costs exist but aren't prohibitive. Customers can easily order from competitors if price/quality diverges. However, convenience and habit create meaningful friction.

### Regulatory/IP Protection: 1/3 (WEAK)
- **Proprietary technology** - DomOS platform, AI ordering, GPS tracking
- **Trade secrets** - Dough recipes, supply chain optimization
- **Limited patent protection** - Some technology patents but easily worked around

**Weakness:** Restaurant industry has low regulatory barriers. Technology provides advantage but isn't legally protected. Competitors can imitate (as seen with current promotional environment).

### Data Moat: 3/3 (STRONG)
- **70%+ digital ordering penetration** - Massive customer data collection
- **37.3M loyalty members** - Transaction history, preferences, frequency
- **AI-powered personalization** - Predictive ordering, targeted promotions
- **Supply chain optimization** - Demand forecasting across 21K stores
- **Proprietary DomOS platform** - Real-time operations data

**Evidence:** $150M+ annual technology investment creating compounding data advantage. Digital ordering data enables personalized marketing, optimized inventory, and dynamic pricing that competitors (especially independent pizzerias) can't match.

**Total Moat Score: 14/18** - Strong economic moat with particular strength in brand, cost advantages, and data. Weakest in regulatory protection, but this is typical for restaurants.
