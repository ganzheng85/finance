3. Current Issues Analysis

Issue #1: Structural Beer Demand Softening Among Hispanic Consumers

  • Category: Revenue / External
  • Severity: 4/5 - High Impact: Hispanic consumers drive 40%+ of premium Mexican beer consumption; "more pronounced" weakness per management threatens core growth engine
  • Urgency: 4/5 - Near-Term: Modelo Especial volume -3% FY26 (first decline ever); Q1 FY27 showed no improvement
  • Trend: Worsening - Q4 FY26 saw improvement (+0.6% depletions), but Q1 FY27 reversed (-3% net sales YoY)

Description:
Constellation is experiencing unprecedented demand softness in its core beer business, driven primarily by weakening Hispanic consumer spending. Modelo Especial, the #1 U.S. beer brand and STZ's flagship (estimated 50%+ of beer segment revenue), declined 3% in depletions during FY26 - the first-ever volume decline for the brand. Management cited "more pronounced" buy rate declines for Hispanic consumers compared to general market, with both trip frequency and spend per trip falling.

Root Causes:
1. Macroeconomic Pressure on Hispanic Households: Elevated inflation, higher interest rates, and economic uncertainty disproportionately impacting Hispanic consumers (lower median income vs general population)
2. Market Saturation: Modelo Especial may have reached peak penetration after 15+ years of uninterrupted growth; distribution already 95%+ in U.S. tracked channels
3. Moderation Trends: Health consciousness and "mindful drinking" reducing per-capita beer consumption, especially among younger consumers
4. Competitive Fragmentation: Hard seltzers, RTD cocktails, cannabis products competing for Hispanic consumer wallet share

Impact:
- FY26: Beer net sales -3% ($8.32B), depletions -2.1%, shipments -3.8%
- Q1 FY27: Net sales -3% YoY despite pricing +3-4% (implies volume -6-7%)
- Margin Pressure: Fixed cost deleverage from lower volumes compressed margins
- Guidance Cut: FY27 organic net sales growth -1% to +1% (down from prior +3-5% expectations); FY28 guidance withdrawn entirely

Why This Matters:
If Hispanic consumer weakness is cyclical (economic), recovery possible as macro improves. If structural (preference shift, saturation), STZ faces years of low/no growth in 90%+ of business. Current stock price ($130, 11.1x forward P/E) suggests market pricing in structural scenario.

Issue #2: Aluminum Tariff Margin Compression

  • Category: Operational / Margin / External
  • Severity: 3/5 - Moderate Impact: 340 bps margin hit in Q4 FY26, but partially offset by pricing power
  • Urgency: 3/5 - Medium-Term: Tariffs implemented June 2025, full impact ongoing through FY27
  • Trend: Stable - Tariff rate locked at 50%; margin impact quantified and being managed

Description:
U.S. aluminum tariffs increased from 25% to 50% in June 2025, significantly impacting Constellation's cost structure. STZ packages approximately 41% of Mexican beer production in aluminum cans (vs 59% glass bottles), with all aluminum imported from Mexico. The tariff increase created immediate margin pressure, contributing to 340 basis point operating margin compression in Q4 FY26 (to 33.2% from ~36.6% prior year).

Root Cause:
- U.S. trade policy shift to incentivize domestic aluminum production
- STZ's Mexican brewing operations require imported aluminum (limited domestic Mexico supply)
- Cannot easily switch to U.S. aluminum (logistics, supply chain complexity)

Impact:
- Q4 FY26: Beer operating margin 33.2% (down 340 bps); ~$100M estimated tariff cost
- FY26 Full Year: Beer operating margin 38.0% (down 170 bps from ~39.7%)
- FY27 Guidance: Beer operating margin 37-38% (implies continued 100-200 bps headwind)
- Cash Impact: Estimated $20M incremental cost FY26, scaling to $80-100M annually at 50% tariff rate

Management Response:
- Pricing Actions: Implemented 3-4% pricing increases in FY26 to partially offset tariff costs
- Mix Shift: Promoting higher-margin glass bottles (Pacifico, Victoria) which aren't subject to aluminum tariffs
- CapEx Investment: $1B capital plan in Mexico to improve efficiency and potentially shift packaging mix
- Cost Reduction: Organizational restructuring targeting $200M+ net cost savings by FY28

Why This Matters:
Tariffs are likely permanent (bipartisan U.S. manufacturing policy support), representing structural margin headwind. STZ must offset through pricing (risks volume) or productivity (requires time/capEx). Current 38% beer margins remain world-class, but trajectory from 40%+ (historical) to 37-38% (FY27) represents significant profit dollar erosion if volumes don't recover.

Issue #4: Massive Capacity Expansion Depreciating into Weak Demand

  • Category: Operational / Strategic
  • Severity: 3/5 - Moderate Impact: Depreciation pressure on margins, but capEx declining FY27+
  • Urgency: 2/5 - Medium-Term: New Veracruz brewery coming online mid-FY27, adding depreciation at worst time
  • Trend: Worsening Short-Term, Stabilizing Long-Term - Depreciation headwind through FY28, then moderates

Description:
Constellation invested $5B+ in Mexican brewery capacity expansion from 2020-2026, adding ~30M hectoliters of capacity in anticipation of continued Modelo/Corona growth. This includes a massive new brewery in Veracruz (southeastern Mexico) scheduled to begin production mid-FY27. The timing is problematic: capacity coming online just as demand is declining, creating fixed cost deleverage and margin pressure.

Root Cause:
- Planning Lag: Brewery capacity decisions made 3-5 years in advance; FY26 weakness wasn't foreseeable in 2021-2022 planning cycle
- Optimistic Growth Assumptions: Expansion planned for 5-7% annual beer depletion growth; actual FY26 was -2.1%
- Strategic Commitment: Once construction began, economically rational to complete vs abandon

Impact:
- Q4 FY26 Margins: Incremental depreciation contributed to 340 bps margin decline (alongside tariffs)
- FY27 Guidance: Beer operating margin 37-38% reflects continued depreciation pressure as Veracruz ramps
- Utilization Rate: Estimated 75-80% capacity utilization FY27 vs target 85-90%, creating inefficiency
- Balance Sheet: $5B+ capEx funded partly by debt; net debt/EBITDA estimated ~3.5x (elevated for STZ)

Management Response:
- CapEx Reduction: FY27 capEx guidance cut to $1.3-1.5B (down from $1.8-2.0B in FY26) as expansion completes
- Operational Efficiency: Targeting $200M+ cost savings by FY28 to offset fixed cost deleverage
- Long-Term Positioning: Management argues capacity will be needed by late 2020s if demand recovers to 2-3% growth
- Optionality: Additional capacity enables contract brewing, new brand launches, or product extensions (non-alc, flavored)

Why This Matters:
Short-term (FY27-FY28), excess capacity depresses margins and creates cash flow headwind. Long-term (2028+), capacity could be strategic advantage if demand recovers or STZ pursues M&A/new products. Current stock price likely discounting "stranded asset" scenario where capacity never fully utilized. If beer depletions return to even +1-2% growth, capacity utilization improves and margins re-expand to 39-40%+.
