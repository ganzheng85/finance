## 3. Current Issues Analysis

### Issue #1: Valuation Risk (Premium Multiple Sustainability)
- **Category**: Financial / External
- **Severity**: 4/5 - Directly limits upside; any disappointment = sharp multiple compression
- **Urgency**: 3/5 - Not immediate crisis, but ongoing constraint on returns
- **Trend**: Stable (oscillating between 47-56x P/E based on sentiment)
- **Description**: Costco trades at 50-56x forward P/E vs 10-year average of 37x and sector average of 22x. The 35-51% premium to history reflects quality, but leaves no margin for error. If sales growth decelerates from 9-12% to 6-8%, or if margins compress 10-20bps, multiple could de-rate to 40-42x (15-20% downside).
- **Impact**: Limits upside to ~15-20% under base case (vs 30-40% if trading at historical 37x). Creates asymmetric risk profile: Small miss = large downside, large beat = small upside (already priced).

### Issue #2: Tariff Passthrough and Refund Execution
- **Category**: Operational / External
- **Severity**: 2/5 - Minor margin pressure, but manageable
- **Urgency**: 3/5 - Refund timing uncertain (Q4 FY26 or Q1 FY27?)
- **Trend**: Improving - Management committed to refunding members, tariff headwinds easing
- **Description**: 2026 tariffs on Chinese imports temporarily raised costs. Costco passed costs to members (raised prices modestly), but committed to refunding members when tariff relief materializes. This creates short-term margin pressure (absorbed costs while awaiting refunds) and member trust test (will refunds actually happen?).
- **Impact**: Estimated 5-10bps EBITDA margin headwind in Q3-Q4 FY26 (~$140-280M annual impact). Stock sold off 7% in April on tariff news, recovered 5% as refund commitment communicated. Trust test: If refunds don't materialize, renewal rates could tick down 0.5-1.0% (minor but symbolically damaging).

### Issue #3: Digital Sales Profitability Unclear
- **Category**: Strategic / Margin
- **Severity**: 3/5 - Growing fast (21.5% growth) but unclear if profitable at scale
- **Urgency**: 2/5 - Long-term issue; doesn't pressure near-term results
- **Trend**: Stable - Digital growing, but management hasn't disclosed unit economics
- **Description**: E-commerce sales accelerating (+21.5% in Q3), driven by AI personalization and app traffic +37%. This is strategically positive (omnichannel future), but management hasn't disclosed whether digital sales are profitable, breakeven, or dilutive to overall margins. Fulfillment costs (Instacart partnerships, delivery logistics) are structurally higher than in-warehouse pickup. If digital scales to 20-25% of sales at negative margins, could compress overall profitability.
- **Impact**: Unknown. Best case: Digital achieves 2-3% EBITDA margins (in-line with Amazon retail) as scale improves. Worst case: Digital loses money, drags overall margins down 20-30bps as mix shifts. Management needs to disclose digital unit economics by FY27.

### Issue #4: International Expansion Execution Risk
- **Category**: Strategic / Operational
- **Severity**: 2/5 - Upside opportunity more than downside risk
- **Urgency**: 1/5 - Long-term growth driver, not near-term pressure
- **Trend**: Improving - 26 net new warehouses in FY26, targeting 30+/year going forward
- **Description**: Costco targets 30+ warehouse openings/year (vs historical 20-25). International (especially China, Japan, Korea) presents massive TAM, but also execution risk: Cultural fit (warehouse shopping not universal), real estate (finding sites in dense Asian cities), supply chain (local vendor relationships), competition (Alibaba's Freshippo in China). If international openings underperform (lower sales/sq ft, lower renewal rates), returns on $6.5B annual capex decline.
- **Impact**: Management guides $6.5B FY26 capex (~2.2% of revenue). If international warehouses generate 70% of US productivity (vs 85-90% target), ROIC drops from 17-18% to 14-15%. Not a disaster, but below-average capital allocation.

### Issue #5: Labor Cost Inflation
- **Category**: Margin / Operational
- **Severity**: 2/5 - Ongoing headwind, but manageable via membership fee increases
- **Urgency**: 2/5 - Slow-burn issue, not acute
- **Trend**: Worsening - US wage inflation 3-4%/year, Costco pays premium wages
- **Description**: Costco's employee-friendly culture (avg $27/hr, top-tier benefits) is a moat driver (low turnover, better service), but also a cost structure vulnerability. As minimum wages rise and labor markets tighten, wage expense grows faster than revenue. Costco can't cut wages without damaging culture (and moat). Mitigation: Membership fee increases (last raised 2024, $55→$65) and automation (self-checkout, inventory robotics).
- **Impact**: Labor = ~70% of SG&A. If wage inflation runs 3-4%/year and revenue growth slows to 6-7%, SG&A leverage disappears. Could compress EBITDA margins 5-10bps/year unless offset by membership fee hikes or automation. Long-term, this forces another membership fee increase in 2027-2028 (to $70-75), which risks churn.

### Issue Summary
- **Total Critical Issues** (Severity 4-5): 1 (Valuation Risk)
- **Most Urgent**: Tariff refund execution (near-term member trust test)
- **Most Severe**: Valuation multiple sustainability (limits upside, creates downside risk)
- **Biggest Risk**: If growth slows to 6-8% AND valuation de-rates to 40x, stock could fall 20-25%

**Overall**: Costco's "issues" are primarily **valuation-driven**, not operational. The business is executing flawlessly (9-12% revenue growth, 92% renewals, 21% digital growth). The challenge is the stock price has run ahead of the business, creating a setup where perfection is priced in and any stumble triggers outsized selling. This isn't a "broken company needs fixing" situation - it's a "great company, rich valuation" situation.

1. **Comp sales growth sustains at 6-8%**: If slows to 3-5% (consumer spending weakness), revenue growth falls to 7-9% → EPS growth 8-10% → multiple de-rates to 42-45x → stock to $850-920. **Sensitivity: High**.

5. **Digital scales profitably**: If digital loses money at scale, margins compress 20-40bps → EPS growth slows to 8-10% → multiple de-rates → stock to $870-950. **Sensitivity: Medium**.
