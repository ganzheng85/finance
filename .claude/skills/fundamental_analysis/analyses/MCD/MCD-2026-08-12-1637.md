## 2. Moat Analysis: Total Score 14/18

| Moat Type | Score | Evidence |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| Brand Power | 3/3 | Top-10 global brand (~$190B value); Golden Arches recognized worldwide; pricing power in value segment |
| Network Effects | 2/3 | Franchise network creates scale; 210M loyalty app users drive personalized engagement; moderate network effects |
| Cost Advantages | 3/3 | Largest beef/potato buyer globally; 57.4% gross margin vs industry 45-50%; unmatched procurement scale |
| Switching Costs | 1/3 | Low customer switching costs (fast food is commoditized); franchise agreements create some lock-in |
| Regulatory/IP | 2/3 | Strong trademark portfolio (Golden Arches, Big Mac); franchise model creates regulatory moat; moderate IP protection |
| Data Moat | 3/3 | 220M loyalty records pooled into global AI dataset; geofencing/Ready on Arrival; predictive maintenance via Google Cloud |

### Moat Strength: Strong

**Key Moat Drivers**:
1. **Franchise & Real Estate Model**: Owns/leases 55% of franchise locations, collecting both 4-5% royalties AND rent. This dual revenue stream generated 46.1% operating margin in FY2025—far superior to company-operated restaurant economics. Asset-light model enables rapid scaling with low capital intensity.

2. **Unmatched Scale & Procurement Power**: As the world's largest buyer of beef and potatoes, McDonald's achieves cost advantages competitors cannot replicate. 57.4% gross margin vs sub-industry average of 45-50% demonstrates structural cost advantage.

3. **Brand Recognition & Global Reach**: Interbrand values McDonald's brand at ~$190B, ranking it a top-10 global brand. Serves 70 million customers daily across 100+ countries. The Golden Arches are among the most recognized symbols globally.

4. **Data & Technology Integration**: 220M loyalty app users generate proprietary behavioral data pooled into a global AI training dataset (partnership with Google Cloud). Dynamic Yield adjusts menu boards based on weather/time/trending items, directly lifting average check. Ready on Arrival geofencing reduces wait times by 50%+.

### Moat Durability: Stable with Technology Strengthening

**Moat Strengthening Factors**:
- Digital/loyalty platform creating new switching costs (doubled sales vs 2023; customers who join more than double visit frequency in first year)
- AI integration via Google Cloud Edge platform rolling out globally (predictive maintenance, faster crew decisions)
- Real estate ownership provides durable competitive advantage and inflation hedge

**Moat Threats**:
1. **Fast-Casual Competition**: Chipotle, Shake Shack, Sweetgreen attracting younger, affluent customers with premium positioning. These chains view McDonald's as "fallback option" rather than destination.
2. **Low Customer Switching Costs**: Unlike enterprise software, consumers can easily switch to Burger King, Wendy's, or fast-casual alternatives with zero friction.
3. **Execution Vulnerability**: Brand power erodes if service quality, speed, or value perception deteriorates (as seen in Q2 2026).
4. **Digital Disruption**: Third-party delivery platforms (DoorDash, Uber Eats) commoditize restaurant choice and reduce direct customer relationships.

### Competitive Position vs Peers

**vs Yum Brands (YUM)**: YUM has higher operating margins (31.08% vs MCD's similar levels) and 1.37% dividend yield. Both convert sales to profit at top-quartile rates. McDonald's has stronger brand power and larger scale.

**vs Restaurant Brands (QSR)**: QSR offers higher dividend yield (3.43% vs MCD's 2.71%) and trades at lower valuations. MCD has superior brand strength and U.S. market position.

**vs Chipotle (CMG)**: Chipotle cultivates premium brand allowing higher prices without losing traffic. Fast-casual model targets different customer segment. CMG returned to traffic growth in Q1 2026 while MCD struggled.

**vs Starbucks (SBUX)**: Both face traffic challenges; SBUX down 16% in past year vs MCD -11%. MCD has more diversified menu and daypart mix.

## 3. Current Issues Analysis

### Issue #1: U.S. Execution Breakdown & Traffic Declines
- **Category**: Operational / Strategic
- **Severity**: 4/5 - U.S. is largest market; traffic down 4.6% YoY in Q2 is significant
- **Urgency**: 4/5 - Must fix within 2-3 quarters or risk sustained market share loss
- **Trend**: Worsening (U.S. comps decelerated from 2.5% in Q2 2025 to 0.8% in Q2 2026; July slightly negative)
- **Description**: Management acknowledged U.S. restaurants inconsistently executed value strategy, pulled back digital offers too aggressively (alienating loyalty members), and deployed too many operational changes simultaneously, overwhelming crews and degrading service times/customer satisfaction. Customer visits declined 4.6% YoY in Q2, with May particularly weak.
- **Impact**: U.S. same-store sales growth of only 0.8% in Q2 vs 2.5% prior year. Traffic declines directly impact profitability as fixed costs remain while customer counts fall. Analyst price target cuts reflect lost confidence.

### Issue #2: Margin Pressure from Labor & Commodity Inflation
- **Category**: Margin / External
- **Severity**: 3/5 - Company-operated margins "not acceptable" per management
- **Urgency**: 3/5 - Structural cost pressures ongoing
- **Trend**: Stable to Worsening
- **Description**: Fast food wages increased from $10-11/hour in 2020 to $14-16/hour in 2026, with California setting $20 minimum for fast food workers. Company-operated U.S. margins compressed as restaurants invested in additional labor while restraining pricing. Food and paper inflation expected in low- to mid-single digits for U.S., mid-single digits for International Operated Markets in 2026.
- **Impact**: Company-operated restaurant margins compressed significantly. Management evaluating whether to refranchise underperforming company-operated stores to reduce direct labor cost exposure. Only ~5% of U.S. locations are company-operated, limiting total impact.

### Issue #3: Fast-Casual Competitive Pressure
- **Category**: Competitive / Strategic
- **Severity**: 3/5 - Gradual market share erosion among younger demographics
- **Urgency**: 2/5 - Long-term threat but not immediate crisis
- **Trend**: Worsening (fast-casual market growing at 14% CAGR 2026-2030)
- **Description**: Shake Shack, Sweetgreen, Chipotle, and other fast-casual chains are attracting younger, affluent customers who view McDonald's as fallback option rather than destination. Chipotle's premium positioning allows higher prices without losing traffic. Shake Shack reported positive same-store sales and traffic in Q1 2026 with 17 new openings. Fast-casual segment growing at 14% CAGR while QSR growth more mature.
- **Impact**: McDonald's maintains 28% top-of-mind awareness vs Chipotle's 68% aided recall among certain segments. Risk of becoming "old brand" for next generation of consumers despite digital transformation efforts.

### Issue #4: International Growth Slowdown & Currency Headwinds
- **Category**: Revenue / External
- **Severity**: 2/5 - International still growing but decelerating
- **Urgency**: 2/5 - Long-term growth driver but not immediate concern
- **Trend**: Stable to Improving (International Developmental Licensed Markets +1.9% comps in Q2)
- **Description**: Company lowered foreign currency EPS tailwind estimate from $0.20-0.30 to $0.15 for full-year 2026. Target of 50,000 global restaurants pushed from 2027 to 2028 due to "cumulative inflation and constrained consumer environment." International Operated Markets comp growth only 1.5% in Q2.
- **Impact**: Slower unit growth reduces long-term revenue trajectory. FX headwinds compress reported earnings. International markets represent significant portion of portfolio and future growth.

### Issue #5: Consumer Budget Pressure & Value Perception
- **Category**: External / Revenue
- **Severity**: 3/5 - Impacts traffic and transaction size
- **Urgency**: 3/5 - Ongoing macroeconomic headwind
- **Trend**: Stable (consumer remains pressured but stabilizing)
- **Description**: Cumulative inflation since 2020 has pressured consumer budgets. McDonald's raised prices significantly during 2021-2023 inflationary period. Customers increasingly price-sensitive, comparing value across QSR and fast-casual. Inconsistent execution of value platform in U.S. confused customers.
- **Impact**: Traffic declines (-4.6% U.S.) reflect consumer trade-down behavior and value-seeking. Requires careful balance of pricing and value perception to maintain traffic while preserving margins.

### Issue Summary
- **Total Critical Issues** (Severity 4-5): 1 (U.S. Execution)
- **Most Urgent**: U.S. execution breakdown—must stabilize within 2-3 quarters
- **Most Severe**: U.S. execution breakdown—directly impacts largest market
- **Biggest Risk**: Sustained traffic declines become structural rather than temporary execution problem, indicating permanent market share loss to fast-casual
