================================================================================
QUICK MENTAL MODEL
================================================================================

Think of sectors like students in a class:

RS (20d) = Last month's test score
  - Who did well on the most RECENT test?
  - Shows who's "hot" right now

RS (60d) = Semester average
  - Who's been doing well all semester?
  - Shows who's consistently strong

Momentum = Year-to-date average
  - Who's been trending up all year?
  - Shows overall direction

Score = Final Grade
  - Combines everything
  - A+ students (80+) = Buy
  - F students (below 40) = Avoid
